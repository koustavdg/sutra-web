What's inside

Nine things, done properly.

Bring your own server

Connect Kavita, Calibre, Grimmory, or any OPDS-compatible server in a few taps. Works on your local network — no cloud middleman.

Gutenberg & Standard Ebooks

Project Gutenberg's 75,000+ public-domain classics and Standard Ebooks' typeset collection — no server required. Patrons Circle sign-in unlocks the full Standard Ebooks catalog.

EPUB, PDF & mobi

Authentic page-curl, rich typography controls, four reading themes, bookmarks, in-book search, and color-coded highlights synced via iCloud.

Panel-aware comics

Sutra detects each comic page's panels and reads them one at a time — tap or flip to advance panel by panel. Works on fixed-layout EPUBs and PDFs. Falls back gracefully to the full page when needed.

Auto-scrolling

Set your pace and let the page flow — tap to pause, tap again to resume. Speed is adjustable from the reading settings. Perfect for hands-free reading.

Sideload local files

Open EPUB and PDF files directly from your phone — from your Files app, AirDrop, or any share sheet. No server required.

Highlights & Dictionary

Select text to highlight with color, double-tap a word to define it instantly — both sync across your devices via iCloud and are saved for later.

Pick up where you left off

iCloud syncs reading position, bookmarks, and highlights across all your devices. Kavita sync is page-accurate in both directions.

Browse by series

Books organize by series in the Collections Shelf based on metadata from your server — perfect for long fantasy and sci-fi runs.

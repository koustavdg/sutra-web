What's inside

Twelve things, done properly.

Both

Bring your own server

Connect Kavita, Calibre, Komga, or any OPDS server, plus WebDAV hosts like Synology. Everything works on your local network, with no cloud middleman.

Both

Gutenberg & Standard Ebooks

Project Gutenberg's 75,000+ public-domain classics and Standard Ebooks' typeset collection, no server required.

iOS

A library that sorts your way

Sort by Recently Read, Title, Author, or Date Added. Books with no artwork get a clean title-and-author cover instead of a blank tile.

iOS

Series, Collections & Favorites

Series appear as grid tiles, and books from the same series stack with a count. Collections open in a consistent grid, and you can rename your own. Favorites is built in, always first, with its own heart-marked card.

Both

EPUB, PDF, CBZ & MOBI

Themes, fonts, bookmarks, and in-book search. Open files from your device's file manager or any share sheet, no server required. Landscape on iPad and Android tablets shows a two-panel facing-page spread, and iOS adds authentic page-curl turns.

Both

Panel-aware comics & manga

Panel Mode and right-to-left manga reading are available on iOS and Android for supported content. Full CBZ reading requires Pro. CBZ page streaming is available on supported Kavita and Komga setups; Komga also requires the account's Page Streaming permission.

iOS

Auto-scrolling

Set your pace and let the page flow. Swipe up or down to adjust speed on the fly, or tap to pause and resume. Perfect for hands-free reading.

Android

Made for e-ink

A dedicated e-ink display mode keeps reading crisp on e-ink screens.

Both

Highlights, Notes & Dictionary

Highlight in color, add notes, and look up words in the built-in dictionary. On iOS, double-tap a word to define it, and footnotes open in a sheet so you never lose your place.

Both

Pick up where you left off

Reading progress syncs back to your Kavita and Komga servers. On iOS, iCloud also keeps position, bookmarks, and highlights in step across your devices, and on Android, Export keeps your data portable.

Both

Reading Statistics

A Stats tab tracks your reading time and progress across your library. On iOS you also get a streak, a swipeable activity chart, and time-remaining estimates that learn your speed for each format.

Both

Try Before You Buy

A limited preview may be available for supported CBZ and comic-shaped MOBI files. Full CBZ and DRM-free MOBI reading require Sutra Pro.

About Sutra

A reader for your books, your comics, and the libraries you build.

Your library, your way.

Sutra brings your DRM-free books and comics together on iPhone, iPad, and Android. Read files on your device or connect to supported libraries and catalogs, including Kavita, Komga, Calibre, WebDAV, and OPDS.

From ebooks to manga, Sutra is designed to help you enjoy the collection you already have. It supports EPUB, PDF, CBZ, and DRM-free MOBI, with tools like Panel Mode and right-to-left manga reading for supported content.

Made for readers.

Sutra is proudly made in Canada for people who want to read content they own. EPUB and PDF reading are free; full CBZ and DRM-free MOBI reading, along with Komga connections, require Sutra Pro.

Pro is a one-time purchase through the store for the platform you use. Apple and Google purchases are separate. CBR and DRM-protected files are not supported.
